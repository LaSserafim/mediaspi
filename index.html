The process

Four steps, one webcam.

Every reading happens in sequence, on-device — the same order every time, so the model always compares like against like.

01

Face your camera

Pose and 468-point facial landmarks track in real time, fully on-device.

02

Hold for 3 seconds

A stability snapshot captures shoulder tilt, neck angle, and micro-tension.

03

Metrics are merged

Pose and face signals combine into eight biometric readings, updated live.

04

Get a clinical read

A clinician-style AI model turns your numbers into a severity rating and fixes.
