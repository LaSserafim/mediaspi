The process

Four steps, one webcam.

Every reading happens in sequence, on-device — the same order every time, so the model always compares like against like.

01

Face your camera

Pose landmarks track your body alignment in real time, fully on-device via Web Worker.

02

Hold for 3 seconds

A stability snapshot captures shoulder tilt, neck angle, and spinal drift.

03

Metrics are extracted

Pose signals produce five biomechanical readings, updated live.

04

Get a clinical read

A clinician-style AI model turns your numbers into a severity rating and fixes.
