Clinical summary

Mild forward head posture with elevated facial micro-tension. Shoulders are level and stable, but head position drifts under sustained focus — consistent with prolonged screen work rather than a structural issue.

Severity
Mild

Current issues

14.2° neck deviation places the head noticeably forward of the shoulder line, and head stability variance (0.06) suggests difficulty holding a neutral position under load.

Future risks

Left uncorrected, sustained forward head posture increases cervical spine loading and is commonly associated with tension-type headaches within 6–12 months.

Recommendations

  1. Chin tucks — 3 sets of 10, holding 5 seconds, twice daily.
  2. Raise your monitor to eye level to reduce forward reach.
  3. 90-second upper trapezius stretch every hour of screen time.
